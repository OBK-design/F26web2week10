Flamingos are wading birds known for their long legs, curved bills, and vivid pink feathers. Their color comes from pigments called carotenoids in the algae and small crustaceans they eat.

They feed by filtering food from shallow water with their bills, often holding them upside down. Flamingos also gather in large colonies, where they build mud nests and raise their chicks.

Chameleons are remarkable lizards that live in a variety of warm habitats, from leafy forests to dry scrublands. Imagine a small chameleon moving slowly along a branch, its independently swiveling eyes scanning the leaves for an insect while its curled tail helps it keep balance. When a meal appears, it can launch its long, sticky tongue with surprising speed and bring the insect back to its mouth.
